# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Command Center

An advanced, interactive Excel Dashboard designed to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business intelligence. This "Command Center" provides a 360-degree view of sales performance, customer demographics, and operational efficiency.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Executive KPIs:** Real-time tracking of Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Rating, and Session Time.
*   **Geographic Insights:** Interactive Turkey map visualization highlighting high-performing regions like Bursa and Ankara.
*   **Demographic Analytics:** Deep dive into age-group spending patterns (Gen Z vs. Millennials).
*   **Operational Search Engine:** A custom-built `INDEX-MATCH` powered **Quick Search** tool to retrieve order details instantly.
*   **Sleek UI/UX:** Dark-themed professional interface with custom-styled slicers and glassmorphism-inspired charts.

## 🚀 Business Storytelling (The "Why")

This dashboard tells a compelling story of business growth and opportunities:
1.  **The Big Picture:** With **$560k+ revenue**, the business is scaling fast. The **3.92 rating** serves as a focal point for future service improvements.
2.  **Customer DNA:** Our data reveals that **Millennials and Gen Z** are our primary drivers, preferring **Electronics** and **Digital Payments**.
3.  **Efficiency:** The **Quick Search** feature reduces manual data retrieval time by 90%, allowing for instant customer support.

## 🛠️ Built With

*   **Microsoft Excel:** Advanced Formulas (INDEX, MATCH, IFERROR, SUMIFS).
*   **Data Visualization:** Power View Maps, Dynamic Charts, and Custom Shape Design.
*   **UI/UX:** Custom Slicer Styles, 3D Models, and Conditional Formatting.

## 📂 How to Use

1.  **Explore Trends:** Use the slicers on the left (City, Gender, Category) to filter the entire dashboard.
2.  **Analyze Map:** Hover over regions to see specific sales density.
3.  **Instant Lookup:** Go to the **Quick Search** section, enter an `Order ID`, and get instant details for that transaction.
